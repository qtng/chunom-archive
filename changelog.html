Changes to Chunom.org.

15 November 2026

Better Chữ Nôm suggestions while you type

  • You no longer have to type the marks. Type nuoc and 渃 nước appears at once; nam shows nam, năm, nằm, nấm … together.
  • d also finds đ: duong shows dương and đường.
  • Every mark you type narrows the list: nươc keeps only nước, nắm only nắm. The tone can be written anywhere in the word: hoà and hòa give the same suggestions.
  • Order: words written exactly as you typed come first, then shorter before longer, then characters you choose often or that have a meaning, then alphabetical.
  • Works wherever you type Chữ Nôm.
